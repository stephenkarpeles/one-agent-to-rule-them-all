## What shipped

The Beanline waitlist landing page is live at production: a single dark, editorial page aligned with the brief—email signup as the only conversion goal. The page includes a hero with primary waitlist form, three value pillars (roast-to-order, curated origins, flexible delivery), a three-step “how it works,” seasonal origin cards with tasting notes, beta-taster quotes, a repeated signup block with privacy copy, and a minimal footer. Palette is near-black (#0f0e0c), warm off-white text (#e8e4dc), and gold accent (#c4a574), with responsive layout and no build step.
